# Swap-Free / Islamic account
- This status is intended for clients who cannot pay or receive interest for
  religious reasons and is available only after approval.
- The client must have complete, valid KYC, own the account, apply through the
  Client Dashboard, and close all open trades and pending orders first.
- Current eligibility is limited to Standard Lion and Standard Cheetah.
- Nano, promotion, bonus and credit-holding accounts are not eligible.
- Only one active Swap-Free account is allowed per Client ID and KYC-verified
  identity.
- Each eligible position is swap-free for at most seven consecutive calendar
  days. If it remains open beyond seven days, swap is calculated for the entire
  holding period from the first rollover after opening, not only from day eight.
- Availability is subject to residence, law, sanctions, compliance, eligible
  symbols and current ROCO conditions.
